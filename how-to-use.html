TRIDENT • How it works

Make hydration a habit.

TRIDENT is designed to make everyday hydration simple: customize your water, check the bottle, and use the companion app to stay on track.

Step by step

Using TRIDENT

The following workflow follows the product concept described in the NEPTUNE proposal.

Fill the bottle

Fill your reusable TRIDENT bottle with drinking water and securely close the cap.

Add a flavor capsule

If you want flavored water, place a compatible flavor capsule into the dedicated capsule compartment in the cap. Use the flavor system according to the capsule instructions.

Check the display

Tap the display once to view the current time. Tap twice to view the approximate water temperature and estimated amount of water remaining.

Set your hydration goal

Use the companion Neptune application to establish your daily water-consumption goal and personalize your reminder schedule.

Follow your reminders

The app can notify you when it is time to drink water and can show your progress toward your daily goal.

Build your streak

Consistently meeting your hydration goal contributes to a hydration streak. If the required goal is not met, the streak resets.

Remember: the bottle's water-level reading is an estimate, and the temperature reading is approximate. The features described here are part of the academic product concept.
