Core features

Built around three connected ideas.

Flavored water, smart bottle information, and mobile hydration support work together as one system.

💧

Flavor Capsule System

The cap includes a dedicated compartment for flavor capsules, allowing users to customize their drinking experience. Additional capsules can be purchased separately or through a proposed subscription.

⌁

Smart Display

A compact water-resistant display provides information without opening the app. Single touch shows the current time; double touch shows approximate temperature and estimated water remaining.

🌡

Temperature Sensor

A temperature sensor provides an approximate reading of the water temperature. The information is intended as a general indication.

◔

Water-Level Estimate

A separate sensing system estimates how much water remains in the bottle. The measurement is an estimate rather than a precision reading.

Designed for life

From desk to workout.

TRIDENT is aimed at younger consumers, including students, young professionals, and fitness-oriented users who want a more engaging approach to daily hydration.
